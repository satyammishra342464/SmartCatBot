Area tags

Area details supported by UNICEDE/2.

Area tabs (a.k.a. area and subarea codes) vary by country and by version of UNICEDE/2. Some countries support multiple subareas (e.g. CRESTA and District). Not all of the listed subareas are supported for Market Share imports.

Navigate to the UNICEDE/2 version and country listing to view available area and subarea codes.
