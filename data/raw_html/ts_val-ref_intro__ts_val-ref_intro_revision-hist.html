Revision history

Summary of the change history for the Touchstone Exposure Data Validation Reference since its initial release.

Validation reference version Touchstone version Date Revision description
8.0 2025 Summer 2025
  • Updated peril options, construction and occupancy codes, and secondary risk characteristics topics to reflect support for the new Verisk Inland Flood Model for Malaysia and Indonesia.
7.0 2025 June 2025
  • Updated peril options and codes for Verisk Earthquake Model for Mexico, Verisk Typhoon Model for South Korea, Verisk Inland Flood Model for the UK and the Republic of Ireland, Verisk Severe Thunderstorm Model for the United States, and Verisk Bushfire Model for Australia.
  • Added support for secondary risk characteristics (model-dependent), additional construction codes (including marine), and additional occupancy codes (including large industrial facilities and marine storage) to the updated models.
  • Updated defensible space content for wildfires outside the United States.
6.0 2024 June 2024
  • Added Backwater Valve Protection field to "Location building details" (only for Verisk Inland Flood Model for Canada.
  • Added new smoke peril (SM) for the Verisk Wildfire Model for the United States and support for additional SRCs for the fire peril, such as skylights.
5.1 2023 (11.5) February 2024
  • Added "IT IF" to "Location building details" (Floor of Interest, First Floor Height), "Location connection detail fields" (Foundation Type), and "Location surrounding detail fields" (Custom Elevation and Custom Flood SOP). For the "-1" option, added several models. In "Europe perils", added "PFL" to Italy and "Inland Flood" to Perils for Italy.
  • Touchstone now correctly imports exposure data with the Authorized (A) contract status. You can select and view it in the user interface. The imported data produces loss appropriately, both in isolation and in combination with other non-âAâ contracts in a set.
  • Updated "Financial terms" section with NGM terms.

Added topics for Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, and United Arab Emirates (UAE) codes, which will be supported in the Verisk Earthquake Model for the Middle East in Touchstone in 2024. Updated codes for Jordan, Lebanon, and Saudi Arabia.

Updated "Layers" topic with Next Generation Models (NGM) financial terms, such as the Aggregate Limit field and the Aggregate Attachment Point field.

Changed "Gumma" to "Gunma" in Japan prefecture codes. Added note in various places that Touchstone does not support single-day contracts.
4.0 2022 (10.0) June 2022 Updated -1 option in Floor of Interest.

For the Verisk Severe Thunderstorm Model for the United States, expanded support for combinations of construction/occupancy codes for consistency with the Verisk Hurricane Model for the United States.

Updated occupancy code 374 (greenhouses), construction codes 239 and 240 (wind turbines), and the 2000-series construction codes to support Verisk Severe Thunderstorm Model for the United States. The model also supports additional secondary risk characteristics: Gross Area and Certified Structures (IBHS) for wind; Roof Geometry, Roof Deck, Wall Attached Structure, and Gross Area for hail.
