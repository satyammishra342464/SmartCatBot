Marine cargo occupancy codes

Touchstone 9.0 and later support occupancy codes for marine cargo occupancies.

The marine cargo occupancy codes are paired with marine cargo construction codes 2701-2720. Since the Standard Industrial Classification (SIC) codes are not mapped to 1000-series codes, "n/a" indicates that the column is not applicable.

Note: The updated Verisk Typhoon Model for South Korea and Verisk Bushfire Model for Australia released with Touchstone 2025 in June now support codes 1001-1012. The Verisk Inland Flood Model for Malaysia and Indonesia released with Touchstone 2025 in October also support these codes.
Note: For information about supported codes and limitations for specific models, refer to the model documentation that is available on the Client Portal.

For information about models supporting the marine codes in Touchstone, refer to Marine Modeling in Touchstone, which is available on the Client Portal.

Occupancy code Category SIC2-digit Description
1001 Designated Facilities / Vaults n/a Facilities that are designed and built to store special types of cargo, such as explosives. This often includes higher design criteria or special protection measures for contents located in the facility. Vaults and protected storage for fine arts and specie.
1002 Silo n/a A tower structure used to store bulk material.
1003 Liquid Tanks n/a Tanks to store liquid material, usually in the port area.
1004 Gas Tanks n/a Tanks containing gas material, usually in the port area.
1005 Inside Warehouse at Port n/a Non-containerized cargoes stored in a warehouse in the port area.
1006 Containerized Inside Warehouse n/a Containerized cargoes stored in a warehouse in the port area.
1007 Containerized Stacked Outside n/a Containerized cargoes stored in open areas.
1008 Open Lot or Stockpiled Outside n/a Non-containerized cargoes stored in open areas.
1009 At Destination in Warehouse n/a Non-containerized cargoes stored in an inland warehouse.
1010 At Destination Retail n/a Non-containerized cargoes stored as contents in retail spaces.
1011 Museums, Institutions, Public Buildings n/a Fine arts displayed in museums and art institutions.
1012 Retail & Private Buildings n/a Fine arts and specie stored in private buildings or in retail spaces.
